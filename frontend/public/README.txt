Place your Logo.jpg file in this directory.

The login page is configured to display:
- Logo.jpg at the top of the login card
- Background color: #1a1f3a (dark blue/navy - adjust this if your logo has a different background)

To customize the background color to match your logo:
1. Place Logo.jpg in this /public directory
2. If needed, update the background color in LoginView.vue:
   - Find: bg-[#1a1f3a]
   - Replace with your logo's background color
